3 Days 2 Nights Athirapilly Waterfalls & Munnar

3 Days / 2 Nights Athirapilly • Vazhachal • Munnar Dedicated Private AC Cab 1N Athirapilly + 1N Munnar

Tour Overview

Witness the roar of the mighty Athirapilly Waterfalls—fondly called the "Niagara of India"—combined with the tranquil beauty of Munnar's high-altitude tea plantations.

Athirapilly plunges 80 feet through dense Sholayar rainforests and served as the iconic backdrop for major cinematic marvels like Baahubali and Dil Se. From there, ascend to Munnar for pleasant mountain breezes and picturesque valley viewpoints.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Athirapilly Waterfalls | Rain Forest Wonder

65 km / 2 Hours Drive
  • Pick-up from Cochin Airport or Railway Station and direct drive to Athirapilly.
  • Check-in at forest resort overlooking the Chalakudy river or waterfall view.
  • Afternoon trek to the base of Athirapilly Falls and visit nearby Vazhachal rapids.
  • Relax amidst nature and enjoy evening bird watching. Overnight stay in Athirapilly.
02

Day 2: Athirapilly to Munnar | Journey Into the Clouds

135 km / 4.5 Hours Drive
  • Delicious breakfast amidst birdsong, then check-out and depart for Munnar.
  • Drive through scenic rubber and spice estates, halting at roadside tea stalls.
  • Arrive Munnar, check-in, and visit Photo Point and Blossom Hydel Park. Overnight stay.
03

Day 3: Munnar Sightseeing & Cochin Drop-Off

130 km / 4 Hours
  • Morning visit to Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, and Tea Museum.
  • Post lunch, drive back down to Cochin for airport or railway station departure.
