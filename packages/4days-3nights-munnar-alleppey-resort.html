4 Days 3 Nights Munnar & Alleppey Lake Resort Stay

4 Days / 3 Nights 2N Munnar + 1N Alleppey Lake Resort Backwater Views & Shikkara Boat Ride

Tour Overview

Designed for guests who prefer spacious luxury land-based resorts over overnight houseboats! Experience 2 nights in Munnar followed by 1 night at an idyllic waterfront lake resort in Alleppey overlooking Vembanad Lake, complete with swimming pool, Ayurveda center, and complimentary 2-hour motorized Shikkara country boat cruise.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Munnar

  • Pick-up from Cochin, visit Cheeyappara Waterfalls, Valara Falls, and Spice Plantation.
  • Check-in at Munnar Hill Resort. Overnight stay.
02

Day 2: Full Day Munnar Exploration

  • Visit Eravikulam National Park and Tata Tea Museum.
  • Afternoon visit Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, and Kundala Lake. Overnight stay in Munnar.
03

Day 3: Munnar to Alleppey Waterfront Lake Resort

  • Drive to Alleppey and check-in at Backwater Resort.
  • Enjoy sunset Shikkara village boat ride through narrow canals.
  • Relax by the swimming pool or indulge in traditional Ayurvedic massage. Overnight stay.
04

Day 4: Alleppey to Cochin Departure

  • Breakfast at resort, followed by transfer to Cochin Airport / Railway Station.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Inclusions

  • 2 Nights in Munnar + 1 Night in Alleppey Lake Resort.
  • Daily Breakfast included.
  • Complimentary 2-hour Shikkara boat cruise.
  • Private AC Cab with chauffeur for 4 days.
