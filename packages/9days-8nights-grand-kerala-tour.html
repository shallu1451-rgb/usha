9 Days 8 Nights Grand Kerala Odyssey & Kanyakumari

9 Days / 8 Nights Athirapilly + Munnar + Thekkady + Houseboat + Kovalam + Kanyakumari Dedicated Private AC Cab

Tour Overview

The ultimate flagship grand Kerala holiday designed for travelers who wish to experience the entire breadth of Kerala without compromise. From northern Athirapilly waterfalls and high mountain tea estates in Munnar, to the spice wildlife hills of Thekkady, iconic private houseboats of Alleppey, and sun-drenched beaches of Kovalam, concluding at India's southern tip—Kanyakumari.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Arrival Cochin to Athirapilly Waterfalls

Arrival at Cochin, drive to Athirapilly. Visit roaring falls and riverfront. Overnight in Athirapilly.

02

Day 2: Athirapilly to Munnar

Drive to Munnar via spice plantations and Valara waterfalls. Overnight in Munnar.

03

Day 3: Munnar Full Day Sightseeing

Eravikulam National Park, Tata Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point. Overnight in Munnar.

04

Day 4: Munnar to Thekkady (Periyar)

Drive to Periyar. Wildlife lake boat safari, spice garden walk, Kathakali show. Overnight in Thekkady.

05

Day 5: Thekkady to Alleppey Houseboat

Board private A/C houseboat at 12:30 PM. All meals, sunset backwater cruising and night stay onboard.

06

Day 6: Alleppey to Kovalam Beach

Drive south along coastal highway to Kovalam. Relax by Lighthouse Beach. Overnight in Kovalam.

07

Day 7: Kovalam Beach & Sightseeing

Explore Hawah Beach, Samudra Beach, Marine Aquarium, and Ayurvedic wellness massage. Overnight in Kovalam.

08

Day 8: Day Excursion to Kanyakumari

Visit Vivekananda Rock Memorial, Thiruvalluvar Statue, Suchindram Temple, and sunset at Triveni Sangam. Return to Kovalam.

09

Day 9: Trivandrum Departure

Padmanabhaswamy Temple darshan and transfer to Trivandrum Airport / Railway Station.
