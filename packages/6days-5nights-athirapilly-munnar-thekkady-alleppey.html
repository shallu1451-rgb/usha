6 Days 5 Nights Athirapilly, Munnar, Thekkady & Alleppey

6 Days / 5 Nights 1N Athirapilly + 2N Munnar + 1N Thekkady + 1N Houseboat Dedicated Private AC Cab

Tour Overview

The ultimate 6-day comprehensive Kerala expedition covering the thundering Athirapilly rainforest waterfalls, emerald high-altitude tea carpet of Munnar, wildlife rich Cardamom hills of Thekkady, and a fairytale private houseboat cruise in Alleppey.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Athirapilly Waterfalls

Arrival Cochin, drive to Athirapilly. Visit falls and Vazhachal rapids. Overnight in Athirapilly.

02

Day 2: Athirapilly to Munnar

Scenic uphill drive to Munnar. Visit spice plantations en route. Overnight in Munnar.

03

Day 3: Munnar Sightseeing

Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Lake & Dam, Echo Point. Overnight in Munnar.

04

Day 4: Munnar to Thekkady (Periyar)

Periyar lake wildlife boat safari, spice plantation walk, and cultural Kathakali show. Overnight in Thekkady.

05

Day 5: Thekkady to Alleppey Houseboat

Board private A/C houseboat at 12:30 PM. All meals, sunset backwater cruising, and night stay on water.

06

Day 6: Alleppey to Cochin Drop-off

Breakfast on houseboat, transfer to Cochin Airport / Railway Station.
