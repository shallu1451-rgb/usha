3 Days 2 Nights Munnar Hills & Alleppey Houseboat

3 Days / 2 Nights 1N Munnar • 1N Alleppey Houseboat 100% Private A/C Houseboat Dedicated Private AC Sedan

Tour Overview

Experience the two iconic crown jewels of Kerala in one well-paced 3 Days 2 Nights tour: the emerald mist-shrouded peaks of Munnar and the serene palm-fringed backwaters of Alleppey.

Enjoy a night in Munnar's serene tea valleys, followed by an unforgettable overnight voyage on an authentic wooden Kettuvallam private houseboat. Your onboard private chef prepares hot meals including traditional Kerala red rice, sambar, avial, fried pearl spot fish, and evening banana fritters.

Perfect For: Couples, Short Weekend Honeymoon, Quick Rejuvenation
Includes: Houseboat All Meals + Resort Breakfast + Private Cab

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Munnar | Scenic Ghat Road Drive

130 km / 4 Hours
  • Pick up from Cochin Airport / Railway Station and drive to Munnar.
  • Halt at Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls and visit an organic spice farm.
  • Check-in at Munnar resort, relax and enjoy the serene evening mountain breeze. Overnight stay.
02

Day 2: Munnar to Alleppey | Houseboat Backwater Cruise

160 km / 4.5 Hours to Houseboat Jetty
  • Breakfast at resort, followed by morning visit to Mattupetty Dam and Tea Plantation viewpoint.
  • Drive to Alleppey jetty. Board your traditional private A/C houseboat at 12:30 PM with a fresh tender coconut welcome drink.
  • Cruise through tranquil Vembanad Lake canals, paddy fields below sea level, and village waterways.
  • Savor an authentic freshly cooked Kerala lunch, evening tea with snacks, and dinner under the stars. Overnight stay on houseboat.
03

Day 3: Alleppey to Cochin Departure | Morning Backwaters

80 km / 2.5 Hours
  • Wake up to tranquil morning waters with local birds chirping. Enjoy breakfast while cruising.
  • Check-out from houseboat at 9:00 AM.
  • Drive to Cochin, enjoy Fort Kochi sightseeing or local shopping if time permits before drop-off at Airport/Railway Station.
