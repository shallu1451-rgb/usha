7 Days 6 Nights Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey & Kovalam

7 Days / 6 Nights 2N Munnar + 1N Thekkady + 1N Houseboat + 2N Kovalam Dedicated Private AC Cab

Tour Overview

The definitive 1-week Kerala holiday that delivers every landscape the state is famous for! Explore 2 nights in the misty hill station of Munnar, 1 night in Periyar wildlife reserve Thekkady, 1 night in an Alleppey backwater houseboat, and 2 nights at the world-renowned crescent beaches of Kovalam with a day trip to Kanyakumari.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Munnar

Arrival Cochin, drive to Munnar. Waterfalls and spice garden en route. Overnight in Munnar.

02

Day 2: Munnar Sightseeing

Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Lake & Echo Point. Overnight in Munnar.

03

Day 3: Munnar to Thekkady

Drive to Periyar. Wildlife boat safari, spice farm visit, and cultural Kathakali show. Overnight in Thekkady.

04

Day 4: Thekkady to Alleppey Houseboat

Board private A/C houseboat at 12:30 PM. All meals and night stay on backwaters.

05

Day 5: Alleppey to Kovalam Beach

Drive to Kovalam. Check in at beach resort. Relax at Lighthouse Beach and Hawah Beach. Overnight in Kovalam.

06

Day 6: Kovalam & Kanyakumari Day Excursion

Excursion to Kanyakumari (Vivekananda Rock Memorial, Thiruvalluvar Statue, Triveni Sangam) and Padmanabhapuram Palace. Return to Kovalam.

07

Day 7: Trivandrum Sightseeing & Drop-Off

Visit historic Sri Padmanabhaswamy Temple, then transfer to Trivandrum Airport / Railway Station.
