3 Days 2 Nights Cochin to Munnar Hill Escapade

3 Days / 2 Nights Cochin • Munnar • Cheeyappara Dedicated Private AC Sedan Handpicked 3★/4★ Resort

Tour Overview

Escape into the cool, lush tea gardens of Munnar with Usha Tour and Travels' bespoke 3 Days 2 Nights weekend itinerary. Munnar, nestled at 1,600 meters above sea level in the Western Ghats, was once the favored summer resort of the British administration.

Enjoy scenic drives through winding mist-clad ghat roads, marvel at gushing cascades at Cheeyappara and Valara, immerse yourself in aromatic spice farms, and witness rare flora and Nilgiri Tahr mountain goats in Eravikulam National Park. Wrap up your retreat with complimentary historic sightseeing and spice shopping in Cochin.

Best Suited For: Couples, Weekend Getaways, Small Families
Starting & Ending Point: Cochin Airport / Ernakulam Railway Station

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Arrival Cochin to Munnar | Through the Waterfalls

Approx 130 km / 4 Hours Scenic Mountain Drive
  • Warm traditional greeting by your dedicated chauffeur upon arrival at Cochin Airport or Ernakulam Railway Station.
  • Board your sanitized AC vehicle and proceed on an enchanting drive toward the hills of Munnar.
  • En route, halt at the thunderous Cheeyappara Waterfalls cascading across 7 tiers, followed by scenic Valara Waterfalls.
  • Explore an authentic organic spice garden and learn about cardamom, pepper, vanilla, and clove cultivation with local herbalists.
  • Check-in at your selected hill resort in Munnar. Unwind in the cool mountain climate and enjoy a peaceful night stay.
02

Day 2: Full Day Munnar Sightseeing | Tea Hills & Lakes

Eravikulam, Mattupetty & Echo Point
  • Enjoy a wholesome breakfast at your resort overlooking the misty valleys.
  • Morning visit to Eravikulam (Rajamalai) National Park, the natural habitat of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr mountain goat (ticket assistance provided).
  • Visit the famous KDHP Tata Tea Museum to discover the colonial heritage of tea manufacturing with tea tasting session.
  • Post lunch, visit Mattupetty Dam with speed boating options, Echo Point, Kundala Arch Dam lake, and Pothamedu Viewpoint.
  • Evening at leisure to stroll through the Munnar local town market for freshly packed homemade chocolates, eucalyptus oil, and tea varieties.
03

Day 3: Munnar to Cochin Departure | Heritage Cochin Tour

Historic Fort Kochi & Drop-off
  • Relish a hearty breakfast, complete check-out formalities, and start your drive back down to Cochin.
  • Depending on your flight or train schedule, enjoy a complimentary heritage tour of Fort Kochi: Chinese Fishing Nets, St. Francis Church, Jewish Synagogue, and Dutch Palace.
  • Optional boat cruise along Cochin Marine Walkway and shopping for Kerala sarees, spices, and souvenirs.
  • Timely drop-off at Cochin Airport or Railway Station with unforgettable memories curated by Usha Tour and Travels.
