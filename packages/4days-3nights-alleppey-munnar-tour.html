4 Days 3 Nights Alleppey Houseboat & Munnar Hills

4 Days / 3 Nights 2N Munnar + 1N Private Houseboat Most Popular Honeymoon & Family Package Dedicated Private AC Sedan

Tour Overview

The quintessential 4 Days 3 Nights Kerala holiday that combines 2 leisurely nights in misty Munnar with 1 magical overnight stay on an authentic Alleppey backwater houseboat.

With 2 full nights in Munnar, you will have ample unhurried time to explore Eravikulam National Park, tea processing factories, Mattupetty boating, and spice gardens without rushing. Then, surrender to the gentle ripples of Vembanad lake on your private houseboat.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Munnar | Through Spice Hills & Waterfalls

130 km / 4 Hours
  • Meet and greet at Cochin Airport / Railway Station and departure for Munnar.
  • Visit Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls and an organic spice garden.
  • Check-in at Munnar resort, evening at leisure amidst mist. Overnight stay.
02

Day 2: Full Day Munnar Sightseeing | High Altitude Peaks

Eravikulam, Mattupetty & Echo Point
  • Early morning visit to Eravikulam National Park for Nilgiri Tahr sightings.
  • Visit Tata Tea Museum for tea tasting and factory tour.
  • Afternoon visit to Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, and Kundala Lake.
  • Evening shopping for spices and chocolates in Munnar town. Overnight stay in Munnar.
03

Day 3: Munnar to Alleppey | Backwater Houseboat Cruise

160 km / 4.5 Hours to Jetty
  • Breakfast at Munnar resort, followed by drive down to Alleppey backwaters.
  • Board your private luxury A/C houseboat at 12:30 PM.
  • Enjoy freshly prepared authentic Kerala lunch while gently cruising through canals.
  • Candle-light dinner and comfortable overnight stay on the houseboat.
04

Day 4: Alleppey to Cochin Departure

80 km / 2.5 Hours
  • Breakfast on the houseboat as it cruises back to the finishing point jetty.
  • Check out at 9:00 AM and drive to Cochin.
  • Optional sightseeing in Fort Kochi and drop-off at Cochin Airport / Railway Station.
