5 Days 4 Nights Munnar, Thekkady Wildlife & Houseboat

5 Days / 4 Nights 2N Munnar + 1N Thekkady + 1N Houseboat Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary Safari

Tour Overview

The all-time favorite "Kerala Golden Trio": Tea plantations in Munnar, elephant and wildlife boat safaris in Periyar National Park (Thekkady), and an overnight cruise in an Alleppey houseboat. Complete with spice plantation visits and optional Kalaripayattu martial art shows.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Munnar

  • Pick-up from Cochin and drive to Munnar. Waterfalls and spice garden en route. Overnight in Munnar.
02

Day 2: Munnar Sightseeing

  • Eravikulam National Park, Tata Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, and Echo Point. Overnight in Munnar.
03

Day 3: Munnar to Thekkady (Periyar Wildlife)

  • Scenic 3.5-hour drive to Thekkady. Boat safari on Periyar Lake inside tiger reserve. Watch Kathakali dance and Kalaripayattu show in evening. Overnight in Thekkady.
04

Day 4: Thekkady to Alleppey Houseboat

  • Drive to Alleppey jetty. Board private luxury A/C houseboat at 12:30 PM. All meals and night stay onboard.
05

Day 5: Alleppey to Cochin Drop-off

  • Breakfast on houseboat, check-out at 9 AM, drive to Cochin for departure.
