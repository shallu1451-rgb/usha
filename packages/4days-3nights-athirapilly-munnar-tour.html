4 Days 3 Nights Athirapilly Waterfalls & Munnar Hills

4 Days / 3 Nights 1N Athirapilly + 2N Munnar Dedicated Private AC Cab

Tour Overview

A refreshingly green holiday combining 1 night at Athirapilly rainforest waterfalls and 2 nights among the tea gardens of Munnar. Explore Vazhachal rapids, Charpa falls, Eravikulam National Park, tea museums, and Mattupetty lake.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

01

Day 1: Cochin to Athirapilly Waterfalls

  • Pick-up from Cochin and drive to Athirapilly. Visit Athirapilly Falls and Vazhachal rapids. Overnight stay.
02

Day 2: Athirapilly to Munnar

  • Scenic drive to Munnar through plantations. Check-in and relax at hill resort. Overnight stay.
03

Day 3: Munnar Full Day Sightseeing

  • Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, and Echo Point. Overnight stay.
04

Day 4: Munnar to Cochin Drop-Off

  • Return drive to Cochin for departure flight or train.
